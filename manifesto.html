Founding Principles

The NDF stands for adaptive Khalilism: a Nigeria united through reform, prosperity, and federal competence.

  • 28 Administrative Zones for local empowerment
  • 60–70% resource derivation for economic justice
  • Strict anti‑corruption continuity via BIC ethics
  • Modernization powered by technology, AI, and transhuman progress
